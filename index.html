FAQ

Questions you may have.

How long does a website take?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project. Simple websites can be completed faster than larger websites with custom functionality.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. E-commerce functionality can be designed around the products, payment system and workflow required by the business.

Can you add AI to my website?

Yes. Depending on the project, AI chatbots, assistants, search, automation and other AI features can be integrated.

Can you deploy my website?

Yes. I can prepare the website for deployment and help connect it to a hosting platform and domain.

Do I need to pay everything upfront?

Payment arrangements can be discussed before development begins. For larger projects, milestone-based payments may be appropriate.
